Every line is one mission

Each curve starts from a geopolitical country group and lands on the upper arc according to launch date. Together, the lines reveal the rhythm of a decade.

02

A steadier opening

Through 2020, annual launch counts remained close to 100–115 missions. The fan is still relatively sparse.

03

The cadence accelerates

By 2022, launches had climbed to 186 in a single year, and the upper arc begins to fill much more rapidly.

04

A new launch tempo

Activity surged again in 2023–2025, reaching 330 recorded launches in 2025 — more than three times the 2017 count.

05

The United States leads

With 761 missions, the United States represents the largest share of launches in this period. Hover over any highlighted line to inspect the individual mission.

06

China follows

China recorded 522 missions, forming the second-densest stream in the fan and reinforcing the increasingly concentrated launch landscape.

07

The full picture

Return all six groups and the decade resolves into a dense web: a visual record of how dramatically launch activity expanded.

READING THE TREND

Launch activity did not just broaden — it accelerated.

The ArcFlow chart is strongest at showing density and timing. The annual bars make the underlying growth easier to read: launches hovered around one hundred a year at the beginning of the period, then rose sharply after 2021.

Peak activity came in 2025, while 2026 remains a partial year in this dataset.

ADDING GEOGRAPHY

Launches cluster around a relatively small number of sites.

A true world map provides geographic context, while launch-site markers show where missions took place. Marker colour uses the same geopolitical groups as the ArcFlow chart, and marker size reflects launch volume.

The largest clusters sit at Kennedy / Cape Canaveral and Vandenberg in the United States, with major Chinese, Russian, New Zealand, and European sites also shaping the global pattern.

CLOSING WITH OUTCOME

Most missions were successful, but the failures still matter.

The last view closes the story on outcome. It compresses the decade into a simple result profile: overwhelmingly successful overall, with a smaller set of failures and pad aborts.

This section works best as a concise ending rather than a major exploratory view.
